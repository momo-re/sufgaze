From sound to gaze.

  1. Transmit

    Two transducers in a commercial bone-conduction headphone emit 17–21 kHz chirps.

  2. Receive

    A single microphone near the glasses bridge records a mixed response containing acoustic and coupling-dependent contributions.

  3. Estimate

    CorrFormer receives cross-correlation representations from eight consecutive sensing frames and estimates yaw and pitch.

Tobii provides supervision during data collection; it is not an input to runtime prediction. The measurements do not isolate a pure surface-wave mechanism.
